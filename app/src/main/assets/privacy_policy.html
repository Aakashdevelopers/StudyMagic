Privacy Policy

Exam Uplift | Developed by AM Studio
Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Exam Uplift ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing a rich educational platform featuring interactive quizzes, full-length mock test series, study notes, PDF learning materials, and AI-powered test performance analysis.

This Privacy Policy outlines how information is collected, used, processed, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and associated services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information required to deliver high-quality educational features and personalized performance insights:

  • Quiz & Test Performance Data: Information regarding your test attempts, scores, time spent, accuracy percentages, and subject/topic strengths and weaknesses.
  • AI Analysis Prompts & Response Data: Subject-wise question performance data sent to our AI service provider to generate detailed Mock Test performance insights.
  • Device & Network Information: Basic device parameters (e.g., OS version, device model) and network state required for reliable API connections and content fetching.
  • Local Storage & PDF Caching: Downloaded study notes and PDF educational materials stored locally on your device for fast access and offline reading.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data exclusively for educational and application functionality purposes:

  • To display quiz results, accuracy scores, and answer explanations.
  • To generate automated AI performance breakdowns and study strategies for Mock Tests.
  • To synchronize exam categories, subjects, chapters, topics, tests, and study materials from our cloud database.
  • To maintain application security, troubleshoot bugs, and improve user interface performance.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Exam Uplift uses trusted third-party service APIs to power backend database operations, media loading, and AI analysis. These third parties receive only data required to perform their specific operations:

  • Supabase: Secure cloud database and storage infrastructure used to fetch exam categories, quiz content, test series, and study notes.
  • RapidAPI / AI API Services: Provider used to process mock test performance statistics and return personalized study recommendations and performance summaries.
  • Picasso: Used for secure and efficient image loading within quiz questions and educational materials.
  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android operating system capabilities and app distribution.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Data Security & Retention

We enforce industry-standard technical measures to protect your data during transmission and processing. All network communication with backend APIs occurs over encrypted HTTPS channels. However, please note that no internet transmission method or electronic storage is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Exam Uplift is an educational preparation application designed for students and candidates preparing for academic and competitive examinations. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe child data has been inadvertently provided, please contact us for immediate removal.

7. User Choices and Data Control

You maintain full control over the local data stored on your device:

  • Clear Cached Data: You can clear downloaded notes, cached PDFs, and local app data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Exam Uplift > Storage > Clear Cache / Clear Data.
  • Data Queries & Deletion: You can contact our team to inquire about or request deletion of any associated data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application features or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
