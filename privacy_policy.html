Privacy Policy

Study Magic | Developed by AM Studio
Last Updated: September 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Study Magic ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy while providing a rich educational experience including interactive quizzes, mock test series, study notes, PDF learning materials, and AI-powered study assistance.

This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed by Study Magic when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better educational services to our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Usage and Performance Data: Information regarding how you interact with our app, such as quiz scores, completed test series, study subjects accessed, and app navigation patterns.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection status necessary to ensure smooth content delivery and API synchronization.
  • AI Assistant Inputs: Questions, study queries, or prompts submitted to our AI Study Assistant to generate explanations, answers, and educational content.
  • Storage & Local Data: Locally cached PDF documents, study notes, and user settings stored on your device for offline reading and performance optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Study Magic application and its features.
  • To process and evaluate quiz attempts, display performance analytics, and present exam solutions.
  • To power the AI Study Assistant to provide automated study explanations and guidance.
  • To monitor app performance, detect bugs, and improve overall user interface and content quality.
  • To ensure network security and prevent unauthorized access to restricted features.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Study Magic utilizes trusted third-party service providers to power specific app functionalities. These services may collect information sent by your device as part of API calls:

  • Supabase: Cloud database infrastructure used for syncing exam categories, test series, study notes, and content updates.
  • OpenRouter AI: AI language model provider used to process study prompts and generate instant educational explanations.
  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android platform functionalities, app distribution, and system performance monitoring.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Study Magic is an educational preparation tool intended for students and learners preparing for competitive and academic exams. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete such data.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access and review the data collected about your app usage.
  • Clear locally stored application data and cached PDFs through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Study Magic > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Cache).
  • Request deletion of any account or user data held on our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
